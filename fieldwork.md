# LocalLens – Fieldwork

## 1. Field Visit

- **Location:** Adityapur, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand
- **Visit Date:** 17-11-2026
- **Fieldwork Time:** 10:00 AM – 12:20 PM (shop visits)
- **Total Shop Interaction Time:** 85 minutes
- **Purpose:** To understand how local retailers showcase their products, identify challenges customers face while shopping locally, and evaluate shopkeepers' willingness to list their products on a hyperlocal discovery platform.

I visited three local retail shops in the Adityapur area representing clothing, mobile and electronics, and footwear categories. The discussions focused on product visibility, customer preferences, seasonal demand, and the possibility of displaying products online so customers could discover nearby shops before visiting them.

## 2. Shopkeeper Interactions

### 1. Poonam Fancy Cloth Centre

- **Visit Time:** 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
- **Shop Category:** Men's Wear, Ladies Wear & Kids Wear
- **Principal / Owner (public business directory):** Shankar Kumar Agarwal
- **Other Associated Names:** Komal Nitesh Samantaray and Nitesh Samantaray

**Discussion Findings:**

1. The shopkeeper showed willingness to participate in an initial trial by listing frequently demanded clothing products.
2. Products such as T-shirts, shirts, pants, and seasonal items like hoodies were suggested for online listing.
3. Customers often prefer checking fabric quality, material, and size physically before purchasing.

**Key Observation:**

Product discovery online could help customers identify nearby clothing options, while physical visits would still allow them to check quality and fit.

### 2. New Garg Store

- **Visit Time:** 11:00 AM – 11:35 AM
- **Shop Category:** Mobiles, Electronics & Accessories
- **Proprietor (public business and GST-related records):** Pawan Kumar Agarwal
- **Other Associated Name:** Navin Agarwal

**Discussion Findings:**

1. The shopkeeper agreed with the idea of listing mobile phones on the platform.
2. Warranty and product authenticity are important considerations for customers purchasing mobile phones and electronics.
3. Customer trust plays an important role in influencing customers to visit local electronics stores.

**Key Observation:**

For electronics, customers need more than just price information. Warranty, authenticity, and trust are important factors in their purchase decisions.

### 3. RAW Footwear

- **Visit Time:** 12:00 PM – 12:20 PM
- **Shop Category:** Sneakers, Sports Shoes, Formal Shoes & Loafers
- **Locally Associated Name:** Danish (also referred to as "Danish Bhai" in local promotional videos)

**Discussion Findings:**

1. The shopkeeper suggested listing footwear products on the platform.
2. Seasonal demand, particularly for shoes during winter, was highlighted.
3. Customers prefer physically checking shoe quality, comfort, and fitting before making a purchase.

**Key Observation:**

Online product discovery can help customers explore nearby footwear options, but physical fitting remains important before purchasing.

## 4. Customer-Side Findings

The interactions and observations highlighted different concerns across product categories:

- **Clothing:** Fabric quality, material, and size verification.
- **Footwear:** Shoe quality, comfort, and fitting.
- **Mobile & Electronics:** Warranty, product authenticity, and trust.

These findings suggest that customers may benefit from discovering products and comparing local options online while retaining the ability to inspect products at physical stores.

## 5. Initial Findings

- All three shopkeepers expressed willingness to participate in an initial product-listing trial.
- Shopkeepers suggested products based on customer demand and seasonal trends.
- Product discovery and price visibility could reduce the need for customers to visit multiple shops just to explore available options.
- Physical inspection remains important for categories such as clothing, footwear, and electronics.
- The actual impact of online product visibility on shop visits and sales will need to be measured during the trial.

## 6. Data Collection Method

The information was collected through direct visits and discussions with local retailers in Adityapur. Shop categories, locations, suggested products, and customer-related concerns were noted during the interactions.



## 7. Limitations

- The fieldwork covered only three local retail shops.
- The observations represent initial discussions and should not be treated as findings from a large customer survey.
- Product availability and prices may change; customers should confirm them directly with the shop.
- The effect of LocalLens on actual sales has not yet been measured.
